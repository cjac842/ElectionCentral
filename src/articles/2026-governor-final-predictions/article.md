## Introduction

This year contains 36/50 states holding their Governor races. Let's break down the competitive races to watch tonight.

## Key Races:

### Alaska: Johnathan Kreiss-Tomkins (D) vs. 3 Republicans

![Tomkins](tomkins.png) ![Alaska Republicans](alaska-republicans.png)

The race to replace term limited Governor Mike Dunleavy (R) is definitely interesting. There's only 1 Democrat who finished in the top 4 in the primary, Mr. Tomkins and 3 different Republicans, Bernadette Wilson, Dave Bronson and Treg Taylor. This one may just *accidentally* flip because of Republican ballot exhaustion.

**Rating: Lean D (flip)**

### Arizona: Katie Hobbs (D-Inc.) vs. Andy Biggs (R)

![Katie Hobbs](katie-hobbs.png) ![Andy Biggs](andy-biggs.png)

This is a race that on paper... seemingly got put to bed, which is surprising considering Arizona in 2024 was the swing state that voted for Trump by the biggest margin over Kamala Harris at 5.5%, and Hobbs didn't win by much at all in 2022 against Kari Lake. But polling has her up well above a margin that could cause some fear if she underperforms... she'll likely be ok.

**Rating: Likely D **

### Florida: David Jolly (D) vs. Byron Donalds (R)

![David Jolly](david-jolly.png) ![Byron Donalds](byron-donalds.png)

This race, moreso than the Senate race, could end up much closer than expected. Former Republican now turned Democrat David Jolly is mounting a strong campaign against Representative Byron Donalds to replace outgoing Governor Ron DeSantis (R). Jolly, unlike Angie Nixon in the Senate race, could benefit from crossover support from Republican voters... since he was one prior and also that Governor's races tend to be more *local* and less partisan, while Senate races tend to be much more *national* and more partisan.

Byron Donalds is still favored to win and become the first African American Governor of Florida... but don't rule out what would be a massive upset.

** Rating: Lean R **

### Georgia: Keisha Lance-Bottoms (D) vs. Rick Jackson (R)

![KLB](klb.png) ![Rick Jackson](rick-jackson.png)

This race is essentially a coin flip between former Atlanta Mayor and Biden admin official Keisha Lance-Bottoms and businessman Rick Jackson. Even with Jon Ossoff slated to win by a big margin tonight, Bottoms is locked in a dead heat between her and Jackson due to her "subpar" tenure as mayor of Atlanta, and Mr. Jackson importing millions upon millions of dollars of his personal wealth, like how he did in the primary. He's "outraised" her ~$124M to $5M... a near 25-1 ratio.

This race could be largely dependent on how well Jon Ossoff does, similar to how Rob Sand in Iowa could dictate the Senate race there. If Ossoff does better than his polling indicates, then Keisha could very well be on her way to the Governor's office. If he does worse... then Rick Jackson is in business.

** Rating: Tilt D (flip) **

### Iowa: Rob Sand (D) vs. Zach Lahn (R)

![Rob Sand](rob-sand.png) ![Zach Lahn](zach-lahn.png)

Put this into perspective: 4 years ago, Iowa Republicans dominated election night. Republican Governor Kim Reynolds won reelection by nearly 20pts, Republicans swept the federal races, flipped the attorney general seat that had been held by Tom Miller (D) since 1979, and won every state level office... but one. They failed to defeat Iowa Auditor Rob Sand, and it's coming back to focus now.

Mr. Sand is now running to be Iowa's first Democratic elected Governor since 2006, and with a much different political enviornment than the one he won in 4 years ago, seems slated to flip the Governorship.

But caution is advised... it is a Trump+13 state and Iowa has been known to underestimate Republicans... but Sand's margin over his Republican challenger Zach Lahn is so big that even with a Republican underestimation, he seems to be in decently good shape.

** Rating: Lean D (flip) **

### Kansas: Cindy Holscher (D) vs. Ty Masterson (R)

![Cindy Holscher](holscher.png) ![Ty Masterson](masterson.png)

Kansas has this thing where they go through 2 Republican Governor terms and then 2 Democratic Governor terms, rinse and repeat. Kansas is about to complete Democrat Laura Kelly's Governorship, and while she remains popular in Kansas... it's Kansas.

Roger Marshall's issues in the Senate race seem to be a "Roger Marshall" problem and less of a "Republicans" problem in Kansas, as lately Masterson has been polling quite a bit ahead of Marshall. So even if Marshall loses to Adam Hamilton in the Senate race, Masterson could very well win the Governor's race. A double flip in Kansas between the Senate race flipping Democratic and the Governor race flipping Republican would be crazy.

** Rating: Lean R (flip) **

### Michigan: Jocelyn Benson (D) vs. John James (R)

![Jocelyn Benson](benson.png) ![John James](john-james.png)

While the Michigan Senate race is super competitive, the Governor's race isn't really. Jocelyn Benson is known to be an overperformer and she's facing Representative John Jammes, who's trying for his third statwide bid and his first Governor bid, after losing back to back Senate bids in 2018 and 2020... and it seems like Mr. James will be 0-3 in Michigan statewide races... we call that in hockey a "hat trick".

** Rating: Likely D **

### Nevada: Aaron Ford (D) vs. Joe Lombardo (R-Inc.)

![Aaron Ford](aaron-ford.png) ![Joe Lombardo](lombardo.png)

This race has been relatively quiet compared to some of the other Governor races. Nevada Republican Governor Joe Lombardo is seeking reelection after defeating then-incumbent Democrat Steve Sisolak in 2022. However, his victory is not assured at all, as he's facing a credible challenge from state Attorney General Aaron Ford.

Nevada being a prime state for Democrats to gain in due to Hispanic reversion makes Lombardo's situation... precarious. Democrats have also built up a big lead in the early voting period in Nevada... though out of all the statewide Republican candidates, Lombardo is the best equipt to get crossover support. The question is, what percentage of Democrats is he going to get in a year where there's going to be backlash against the party Lombardo is a part of... and will it be enough considering the big lad NV Dems built up in the EV period?

This one could be an absolute squeeker for either candidate... and with how slow Nevada counts their votes... buckle up we could be stuck on this race for a while.

** Rating: Tilt D (flip) **

### Ohio: Amy Acton (D) vs. Vivek Ramaswamy (R)

![Amy Acton](amy-acton.png) ![Vivek Ramaswamy](vivek.png)

Arguably the nastiest election this entire cycle. This race went beyond ideological battles and went into personal attacks when Vivek launched an ad where Dr. Amy Acton's estranged mother, who allowed Amy to be sexually abused as a child, told voters not to vote for her child. This ad fired up Democrats and frustrated many state level Republicans. Vivek wasn't leading Acton before the ad, but it feels like the ad put him even further behind.

** Rating: Lean D (flip) **

### Oregon: Tina Kotek (D-Inc.) vs. Christine Drazan (R)

![Tina Kotek](tina-kotek.png) ![Christine Drazan](drazan.png)

Ah yes, we're talking about deep blue Oregon being competitive because their Democratic Governor is once again, terribly unpopular. Oregon voters clearly love being subjected to ineptitude from their statewide Democratic Governors because they've been electing these people continuously since 1986! Truely though, Governor Tina Kotek put herself into this position and now is being put on the heels again by the same person she beat in 2022, Christine Drazan.

Drazan will get closer than she should be, but this isn't the year for Oregon Democrats to finally lose a Governor's race... they absolutely will though in the future, and they'd have it coming.

** Rating: Lean D **

### Texas: Gina Hinojosa (D) vs. Greg Abbott (R-Inc.)

![Gina Hinojosa](hinojosa.png) ![Greg Abbott](greg-abbott.png)

Governor Greg Abbott has easily dispatched his Democratic challengers in 2014, 2018 and 2022. This time though it seems that he's in for a much tougher fight to win his 4th term. Gina Hinojosa has polled relatively close to Abbott, but rarely ahead. Governor Abbott is favored to win reelection but this one will **for certain**, be his closest race, whether he wins or loses.

** Rating: Lean R **

### Wisconsin: David Crowley (D) vs. Tom Tiffany (R)

![David Crowley](crowley.png) ![Tom Tiffany](tiffany.png)

Milwaukee County Executive David Crowley was NEVER supposed to be in this spot. After all, he literally DROPPED OUT OF THE PRIMARY at one point. But then scandal hit Lt. Governor Sarah Rodriguez (D) and then Crowley reenetered the primary... but was polling significantly behind DSA aligned Francesca Hong. Everyone thought Francesca Hong was going to be the Democratic nominee, but then the votes were cast... and DAVID CROWLEY won the primary in a garganutuan upset. Meanwhile, his Republican opponent, U.S Representative Tom Tiffany, never faced serious opposition.

Polling in this race has been quite limited, but the latest Marquette poll had Crowley up 7pts over Tiffany... even if there's a miss here it's likely not enough for Representative Tiffany... who basically needed Francesca Hong to be the Democratic nominee to have a chance.

** Rating: Lean D **

## Honorable Mentions

These races aren't really competitive but be on the look out for interesting aspects.

## Minnesota: Amy Klobuchar (D) vs. Lisa Demuth (R)

![Amy Klobuchar](klob.png) ![Lisa Demuth](demuth.png)

Minnesota has been in a state of scandal as of late thanks to former VP candidate Tim Walz being inept, and it's dragging down Klobuchar and Senate candidate Peggy Flanagan. However, it's not enough for Senator Klobuchar to lose and likely not enough for Lt. Governor Flanagan losing her Senate race. Senator Klobuchar will be heading back home to be Governor.

** Rating: Likely D **

### Vermont: Amanda Janoo (D) vs. Phil Scott (R-Inc.)

![Amanda Janoo](janoo.png) ![Phil Scott](phil-scott.png)

Governor Phil Scott has CRUISED to re-election 3 straight times in 2020, 2022 and 2024... winning by bigger margins each and every time. This time as he goes for his 6th consecutive term, there seems to be an angst against him and his Lt. Governor, John Rogers after they scored massive victories in 2024 by Rogers flipping the Lt. Gov office and Scott breaking the Democratic supermajorities in the state legislature. Scott is still favored to win his 6th term, but it will be much closer than his previous elections.

If he wins, don't be surprised if he faces a much more credible Democratic opponent in 2028, or just retire.

** Rating: Likely R **
